"Question:<br />
What does the Semiotic Shift mean in the difference between IT and digitalization?"	The Semiotic Shift describes the transition from fixed, static data representations in classical IT systems to dynamic, complex, and novel digital representations that open up new spaces of meaning and interpretation.
"Question:<br />
What does the Agential Shift describe in the context of digital technologies?"	The Agential Shift describes the shift of agency toward new actors, because digital technologies enable individuals, start-ups, and platforms to drive innovation independently of traditional organizations.
"Question:<br />
What does the Combinatorial Shift describe in the context of digital technologies?"	The Combinatorial Shift describes the possibility of modularly combining digital technologies and thereby creating new products, services, and business models through the recombination of existing digital building blocks.
"Question:<br />
What is meant by the Infrastructural Shift in the context of digitalization?"	The Infrastructural Shift denotes the development from isolated IT systems toward digital infrastructures and platforms that serve as a generative basis for new applications, services, and ecosystems.
"Question:<br />
What theoretical significance does the Agential Shift have for understanding digitalization?"	The Agential Shift expands the agency of technology through autonomous agents and shifts the analysis from purely human-centered control toward hybrid socio-technical interaction systems.
"Question:<br />
How can the difference between IT transformation and digital transformation be theoretically justified through the Economic Shift?"	The Economic Shift establishes that IT transformation primarily addresses efficiency and cost logic, whereas digital transformation changes value creation logics, business models, and market structures, making strategically new revenue mechanisms and ecosystem positioning, tactically new product and data architectures, and operationally new operating and governance mechanisms necessary.
"Question:<br />
